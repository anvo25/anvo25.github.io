An Vo

Research Engineer @ Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)
Incoming PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering @ University of Michigan

I am currently a Research Engineer at MBZUAI working with Thamar Solorio, and an incoming PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at University of Michigan advised by Rada Mihalcea. I am also collaborating closely with Anh Totti Nguyen. Previously, I obtained my MS from KAIST with Daeyoung Kim, where I received the Outstanding Thesis Award and was fully funded by the Hyundai CMK Global Scholarship. I am broadly interested in foundation models, with a focus on:

  1. Trustworthy & Explainable AI: understanding and diagnosing why models fail (e.g., bias, hallucination, robustness, out-of-distribution) through mechanistic interpretability or behavioral analysis.
  2. Multimodal AI: understanding how vision and language interact in large models, particularly grounding, alignment, and cross-modal reasoning.
