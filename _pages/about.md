I am currently a Research Engineer at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/) working with [Thamar Solorio](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/thamar-solorio/), and an incoming PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/) advised by [Rada Mihalcea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rada_Mihalcea). I am also collaborating closely with [Anh Totti Nguyen](https://anhnguyen.me). Previously, I obtained my MS from [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/) with [Daeyoung Kim](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VZezrzcAAAAJ&hl=en), where I received the Outstanding MS Thesis Award and was fully funded by the [Hyundai CMK Global Scholarship](https://www.cmkfoundation-globalscholarship.org/). I am broadly interested in foundation models, especially in making them more trustworthy and explainable in edge/hard cases.
