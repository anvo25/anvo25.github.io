I am currently a Research Engineer at <img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=mbzuai.ac.ae&sz=32" alt="MBZUAI" style="height: 1em; vertical-align: middle; margin: 0 2px;"> [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/) working with [Thamar Solorio](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/thamar-solorio/), and an incoming PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at <img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=umich.edu&sz=32" alt="UMich" style="height: 1em; vertical-align: middle; margin: 0 2px;"> [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/) advised by [Rada Mihalcea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rada_Mihalcea). I am also collaborating closely with [Anh Totti Nguyen](https://anhnguyen.me). Previously, I obtained my MS from <img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=kaist.ac.kr&sz=32" alt="KAIST" style="height: 1em; vertical-align: middle; margin: 0 2px;"> [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/) with [Daeyoung Kim](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VZezrzcAAAAJ&hl=en), where I received the Outstanding Thesis Award and was fully funded by the [Hyundai CMK Global Scholarship](https://www.cmkfoundation-globalscholarship.org/). I am broadly interested in foundation models, with a focus on:
1. **Trustworthy & Explainable AI**: understanding and diagnosing why models fail (e.g., bias, hallucination, robustness, out-of-distribution) through mechanistic interpretability or behavioral analysis.
2. **Multimodal AI**: understanding how vision and language interact in large models, particularly grounding, alignment, and cross-modal reasoning.
